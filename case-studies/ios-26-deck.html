Process · The tension

Apple's material. The merchant's brand.

Liquid Glass wants to be see-through and borrow its color from whatever sits behind it. A merchant's checkout wants to be their color, every time, on every phone.

Liquid GlassApple's material Appearance APIThe merchant's brand
Surface Translucent Opaque
Color Borrowed from the content beneath A brand hex, set once
Owner Apple, per OS release The merchant, per app release
Moves under you Yes. iOS 27 adds a translucency slider for users. Only when the merchant ships
Why not all glass?

A card number over moving content loses contrast, and the merchant's brand washes out to whatever happens to be behind the sheet.

Why not all opaque?

The sheet reads as iOS 18 inside an iOS 26 app, and Apple's floating controls land on top of it anyway.

Process · The reposition

Glass for chrome. Opaque for card data.

[TODO: Lawrence supply the real rule] Placeholder rule, drafted to show the shape of the move.

I stopped asking which material wins. I asked what each layer is for.

Anything that frames the sheet went to the system. Anything that holds a card number, a total, or the merchant's button stayed opaque and stayed theirs. We shipped that split across every surface in the audit.

Let Apple own the frame. Let the merchant own the money.
