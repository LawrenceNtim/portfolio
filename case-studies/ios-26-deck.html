Solutions · Design: Sheet & material

The sheet, pulled apart.

Four layers, one job each. The merchant's app stays alive underneath. Glass carries the material and nothing else. Content gets its own plane, so card data never inherits the blur. Controls sit on top, where the thumb expects them.

I drew the layer model and the rules between layers. We rebuilt PaymentSheet against it, surface by surface.

Solutions · Design: Inputs & legibility

Card data, legible over anything.

A merchant can put anything behind the sheet: a product shot, a gradient, a sale banner. Glass carries that color through. Card data can't afford to.

So fields stay opaque, and labels that sit on glass get a tint dense enough to hold contrast in light and dark. I set the floor. We checked it against the busiest backdrops we could find. [TODO: Lawrence]
