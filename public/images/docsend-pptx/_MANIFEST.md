## Slide 28 — Balancing speed and execution with thoughtfulness and intention, lessons from this.

**Text on slide:**
- Balancing speed and execution with thoughtfulness and intention, lessons from this.
- 1
- Teams are critical
- 2
- Set expectations
- 3
- Data agility
- 4
- Scenario planning
- Reflections

**Notes:** 1: Structuring teams, not only in terms of domain expertise but also unique skillset is necessary (especially for new teams). In addition, building trust and empathy is critical to the process.

2: Expectations of success can be increased when things change. It’s important to focus on taking things step by step and managing what we can control.

3: Data analytics can be challenging, especially when not given prior attention. Evaluating this pre launch will allow for smoother tracking and better decision making,

4: What if everything fails or the behavior is the exact opposite of what we expect? Having contingency plans and backup plans make for a more cohesive planning process. f 28
